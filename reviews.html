Albums listened to lately

MATADEROS by Saracaramala

An Argentine underground artist known for blending trap, alternative hip hop and experimental electronic influences, this is Saracaramala. Her music tends to combine dark aesthetics, unconventional production and expressive style. This album is a dark intense project. The album combines aggressive production, distorted sounds, and haunting atmospheres, creating a rebellious and chaotic listening experience.

NYk0TINE by luKMI

LuKMI is part of Mexico's emerging alternative urban music scene. She doesn't fit into mainstream Latino pop nor traditional reggaeton. This album bring another take on alternative R&B mixing it with trap and Latin R&B.

no me mires así by kuraimokha

This album consists of Chilean trap, plugg and alternative rap. The album reflects a contemporary underground sound. Kuraimokha is a chilean underground rapper known for blending different genres, being one of the newer Latin American underground rap influences with an experimenta, internet-influenced style.

Best Albums

Downward Spiral

by Nine Inch Nails

A groundbreaking industrial rock concept album. Blending distorted guitars, aggressive electronics, and haunting melodies, the album explores addiction, isolation, self-destruction and psychological decline. It's a dark, experimental production and emotionally raw lyrics make it one of the most influetial albums in industrial and alternative rock.

La vida era más corta

by Milo J

An emotionally rich album by Argentine artist Milo J that blends alternative hip hop, Argentine floklore and contemporary urban music. Through poetic lyrics, traditional instrumentation and melancholic melodies, the album explores nostalgia, identity, loss and the passage of time.

Blackstar

by David Bowie

A haunting and experimental final album by legendary british artist David Bowie, blending art rock, jazz, electronic music, and avant garde influences. Through mysterious lyrics, unconventional instrumentation and dark atmospheres, the album explores mortality, identity and acceptance of death.
